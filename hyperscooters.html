The G30 Flagship.

01. Extreme Engineering

Performance in its purest form.

The G30 redefines the power-to-weight ratio. Delivering a massive 32kW (42HP) output in a compact 45kg chassis, it packs half the power of a standard family car into a fraction of the footprint. Engineered for the bold, it achieves a sustained top speed of 140km/h.

02. Deceleration Authority

Surgical Braking Power

Speed is nothing without control. Utilizing a braking system derived from championship-grade German MTB platforms, the G30 offers relentless stopping power. These units are thermal-mapped to handle repeated high-speed shedding from triple-digit speeds without fade.

03. Proven Endurance

Inconspicuous by Design.

With over 3,000km of real-world testing, the G30 platform is built for longevity. Its genius lies in its aesthetic: a rugged, rental-inspired silhouette that provides total mechanical reliability while remaining completely under the radar.

The Performance Reality

Reaching 140 km/h on a small-frame scooter isn't just about raw power—it's about thermal and mechanical harmony. Every build balances voltage, motor gearing, and cooling. My process ensures that batteries and ESCs remain stable under extreme discharge, turning a standard commuter into a stable, high-speed instrument of engineering.
