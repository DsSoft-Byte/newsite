Why VESC?

VESC is open, highly configurable, and engineered for those who demand more. Unlike stock ESCs, you get total control over timing, current limits, and regenerative braking. It is the definitive platform for hyperscooters and bespoke EV projects where performance and safety must coexist under extreme loads.
