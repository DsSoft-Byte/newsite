VESC & high-power ESCs

Modifying and tuning VESC-based electronic speed controllers for extreme performance. Builds that push 450A phase current and 100V nominal are standard for high-speed hyperscooter and DIY EV applications — with proper firmware configuration, thermal design, and integration.

Capabilities

  • Current and voltage: Mods and configurations for 450A+ phase current and up to 100V nominal, matching high-power motors and batteries.
  • Firmware: VESC Tool tuning — current limits, FOC parameters, braking curves, and thermal rollback so hardware stays within safe operating range.
  • Thermal: Heatsinking, thermal pads, and airflow considerations so sustained high current doesn’t cook the FETs or PCB.
  • Integration: Wiring, connectors, and placement in tight frames (Xiaomi, Ninebot, custom) with clean cable runs and strain relief.

Why VESC

VESC is open, configurable, and well-suited to custom builds. Unlike locked-down stock ESCs, you get full control over current limits, timing, and regen. That makes it the go-to platform for hyperscooters and high-performance builds where stock hardware isn’t enough.
