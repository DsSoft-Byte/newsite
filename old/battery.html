Battery building

Custom battery packs for electric vehicles and high-power applications. From cell selection and BMS integration to spot welding and safe assembly — built for hyperscooters, e-bikes, and DIY EV projects that need reliable energy density and discharge capability.

What I build

  • High-discharge packs for hyperscooters and small-frame builds (40S+ configurations where space allows)
  • Custom series/parallel layouts to match controller and motor specs (e.g. 100V nominal for high-power VESC setups)
  • BMS selection and integration: balancing, protection, and communication (UART/CAN where needed)
  • Spot-welded nickel strip (or laser-welded where applicable) for low resistance and durability
  • Thermal and mechanical design: mounting, vibration, and cooling considerations for harsh use

Process

Cell selection is driven by application: energy vs power cells, cycle life, and form factor. I match pack voltage and capacity to your ESC and motor, then design the physical layout for the frame. Assembly is done with proper spot welding equipment, insulation, and strain relief. Every pack is tested for balance, IR, and discharge capability before delivery.
