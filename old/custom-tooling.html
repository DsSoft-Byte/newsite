Custom tooling & products

High-reliability systems and proprietary tooling built for production use: OTA with intelligent rollback, DRM, device management, and autonomous trading systems. Each product is designed to be maintainable and documentable.

FastOTA™

Intelligent over-the-air update system with rollback. Updates are validated before commit; on failure or regression, the system reverts to the last known good image. Designed for embedded and mobile deployments where brick risk is unacceptable.

LayerOne™

Custom DRM and license enforcement for software and digital assets. Multi-layer protection suitable for commercial products; designed with audit and updateability in mind so license logic can evolve without breaking existing deployments.

iOSBridge

Unlocking and device management for iOS hardware. Tied to security research and reverse engineering work — used in authorized contexts for device recovery, testing, and research. Not for circumvention of legitimate ownership controls.

AI trading bot

Autonomous trading system with integrated risk assessment. Strategy logic, position sizing, and risk limits are configurable; the system is built for backtesting and live operation with clear logging and kill switches. For personal and authorized use only.
