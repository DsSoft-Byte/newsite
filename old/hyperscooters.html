Hyperscooters

Building hyperscooters on small, mass-market frames — Xiaomi, Ninebot, and similar — that reach speeds up to 140 km/h. Full custom electronics, battery packs, and VESC-based ESCs tuned for maximum performance in a compact footprint.

What goes into a build

  • Frames: Xiaomi and Ninebot bases are common starting points: robust, available, and well-understood. Mods include reinforcement where needed and mounting for larger batteries and controllers.
  • Power: Custom high-voltage packs (e.g. 72V–100V nominal) and VESC ESCs modified for 450A+ phase current. Motor choice and gearing are matched to target top speed and torque.
  • Firmware & tuning: VESC configuration for FOC, current limits, and thermal management. Throttle mapping and braking curves tuned for street use and safety.
  • Build logs & content: Hyperscooter builds and runs are documented on TikTok — follow for teardowns, speed runs, and build process.

Performance

140 km/h on a small-frame scooter is achievable with the right combination of voltage, current, motor, and gearing. It requires careful thermal and mechanical design: batteries and ESCs need cooling and secure mounting. Every build is done with safety in mind — brakes, tires, and structure are part of the spec, not an afterthought.
