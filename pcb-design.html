PCB design & microsoldering

Production-grade board repair, modification, and custom PCB design. From 0603 components and QFP64 STM32 packages to GPU VRAM replacement and full fabrication pipeline — hardware that has to work at scale.

Advanced microsoldering

  • Component scale: 0603 resistors and smaller; precise placement and rework under magnification.
  • GPU work: VRAM replacement, GPU core rework, and BGA reballing where applicable. Thermal and mechanical care to avoid collateral damage.
  • MCU and packages: QFP64 STM32 and similar packages: removal, replacement, and trace repair. Hot air and soldering station discipline.
  • Board-level debugging: Schematic recovery, continuity, and fault isolation on consumer and custom boards.

Custom PCB design

Full design pipeline from concept to fabrication: schematic capture, layout (including high-current and thermal considerations for power boards), and DFM for assembly. Boards are designed for hyperscooter BMS, custom controllers, and one-off instruments — with clear documentation and revision control.
