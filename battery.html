Hardware Engineering

Battery building

Custom battery packs for electric vehicles and high-power applications. Built for hyperscooters, e-bikes, and DIY EV projects that demand absolute reliability.

Advanced Power Systems

01. High-Density Engineering

Maximum Output. Minimal Footprint.

We specialize in high-voltage, high-density configurations for hyperscooter and custom small-frame builds. By architecting dense 40S+ arrays, we push the limits of energy density, ensuring that peak performance never comes at the cost of thermal stability or safety.

02. Low-Impedance Assembly

Thermal Management & Conductivity.

Our assembly process utilizes custom-engineered, pure copper busbars designed for ultra-low resistance. Precision spot-welding ensures every interconnect is optimized to handle massive 450A+ phase current loads without heat soak, providing consistent power delivery under extreme stress.

03. Systems Integration

Validation & Performance Mapping.

Reliability is the result of rigorous data. Before a single cell is welded, we conduct exhaustive research and stress-testing on cell chemistries and BMS logic. We don't just build packs; we map every component to your specific motor and controller curves to ensure total system synergy.

The Assembly Process

Cell selection is heavily driven by your application: calculating the exact balance of energy vs. power cells, cycle life, and form factor. I match pack voltage and capacity directly to your ESC and motor, then design a physical layout optimized for the frame. Assembly is executed with high-end spot welding equipment, proper multi-layer insulation, and strain relief. Every single pack is tested for cell balance, internal resistance (IR), and sustained discharge capability before delivery.
