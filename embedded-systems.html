Embedded systems engineering

Complete hardware–firmware–software stacks from concept to production. MCU programming, custom protocol design, device recovery, and low-level debugging. When the problem spans silicon, firmware, and runtime, the stack is handled end-to-end.

What I do

  • MCU development: C/C++ on ARM Cortex-M, ESP32, STM32, and similar. Bare metal and RTOS; power and timing constraints are first-class.
  • Custom protocols: Design and implementation of UART, SPI, I2C, and CAN-based protocols for sensors, BMS, and motor controllers. Documentation and interoperability with existing tooling.
  • Device recovery & bring-up: Brick recovery, bootloader repair, and firmware restoration on consumer and custom hardware. Often paired with reverse engineering when specs are missing.
  • Low-level debugging: Logic analysis, oscilloscope, and JTAG/SWD for race conditions, timing issues, and hardware–firmware boundary bugs.

Overlap with other work

Embedded work ties directly into battery management (BMS firmware and comms), VESC tuning and custom ESC logic, hyperscooter dashboards and telemetry, and reverse engineering of firmware and bootloaders. Same discipline: clear requirements, reproducible builds, and documentation that others can follow.
